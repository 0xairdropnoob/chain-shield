Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

🔒 Do I need to connect my wallet?

No. Never. We never ask for wallet connection. Just paste a contract address or wallet address and scan.

👛 What is Smart Wallet Tracking?

Add any whale/trader wallet address. See their full trade history, PnL, win rate, and get real-time alerts when they make moves. Free users can search 1 wallet with limited data.

🚀 Which launchpads do you support?

pump.fun (Solana), gmgn.ai (Solana), four.meme (BNB), bankr.xyz (Multi-chain), and Birdeye. Free users get 1 platform with delayed data. Pro users get all 5, real-time.

💰 What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free gives you the scanner + a taste of smart wallet and launchpad data (limited, blurred, delayed). Pro ($9/mo) unlocks everything: full PnL, real-time alerts, all launchpads, copy-trade triggers, and API access.

📊 Where does the data come from?

GoPlus Security API, DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, on-chain RPC calls, and direct launchpad APIs. Multi-source verification.

⚠️ Can I lose money using this tool?

The tool helps you make informed decisions. It's not financial advice. Always DYOR.
